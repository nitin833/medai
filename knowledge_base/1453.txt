Title: Medicaid

Summary:
Medicaid is government
health insurance
that helps many low-income people in the United States to
pay their medical bills
. The Federal government sets up general guidelines for the program, but each state has its own rules. Your state might require you to pay a part of the cost for some medical services.
You have to meet certain requirements to get Medicaid help. These might involve:
Your age
Whether you are pregnant, blind, or have a disability
Your income and resources
Whether or not you are a U.S. citizen or, if not, what your immigration status is
