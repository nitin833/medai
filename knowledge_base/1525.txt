Title: Syringomyelia

Summary:
Syringomyelia is a rare disorder that causes a cyst to form in your
spinal cord
. This cyst, called a syrinx, gets bigger and longer over time, destroying part of the spinal cord.  Damage to the spinal cord from the syrinx can cause symptoms such as :
Pain and weakness in the back, shoulders, arms or legs
Headaches
Inability to feel hot or cold
Symptoms vary according to the size and location of the syrinx. They often begin in early adulthood.
Syringomyelia usually results from a skull abnormality called a
Chiari I malformation
. A tumor, meningitis or physical trauma can also cause it. Surgery is the main treatment. Some people also need to have the syrinx drained. Medicines can help ease pain. In some cases, there are no symptoms, so you may not need treatment.
