Title: Cloning

Summary:
Cloning describes the processes used to create an exact
genetic
replica of another cell, tissue or organism. The copied material, which has the same genetic makeup as the original, is referred to as a clone.  The most famous clone was a Scottish sheep named Dolly.
There are three different types of cloning:
Gene cloning, which creates copies of genes or segments of DNA
Reproductive cloning, which creates copies of whole animals
Therapeutic cloning, which creates embryonic
stem cells
.  Researchers hope to use these cells to grow healthy tissue to replace injured or diseased tissues in the human body.
