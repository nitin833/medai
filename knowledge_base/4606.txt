Title: Tsunamis

Summary:
A tsunami is a series of huge ocean waves created by an underwater disturbance. Causes include
earthquakes
, landslides,
volcanic eruptions
, or meteorites--chunks of rock from space that strike the surface of Earth.   A tsunami can move hundreds of miles per hour in the open ocean. It can smash into land with waves as high as 100 feet or more and cause devastating
floods
.
Drowning
is the most common cause of death related to a tsunami.
Although there are no guarantees of safety during a tsunami, you can take actions to protect yourself.  You should have a
disaster plan
. Being prepared can help reduce fear, anxiety, and losses. If you do experience a disaster, it is normal to feel stressed. You may need help in finding
ways to cope
.
