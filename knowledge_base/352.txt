Title: Pancreas Transplantation

Summary:
The pancreas is a gland behind your stomach and in front of your spine. It produces the juices that help break down food and the hormones that help control blood sugar levels. A pancreas transplant is surgery to place a healthy pancreas from a donor into a person with a diseased pancreas.  It is mostly done for people with severe
type 1 diabetes
. It can allow them to give up insulin shots.  An experimental procedure called
islet cell transplantation
transplants only the parts of the pancreas that make insulin.
People who have
transplants
must take drugs to keep their body from rejecting the new pancreas for the rest of their lives. They must also have regular follow-up care. Because of the risks, it is not a common treatment for type 1 diabetes.
